## Conventions

- **Columns:** id · oracle · event · context · cue · P · flags · line · chars. Contexts and event names stay in the source language because they are data keys, not player-facing text.
- **Variables** (never the player's username): `{premio}` (prize name with article, up to 30), `{mao}` (hand name, up to 16: "a pair of queens"), `{estreante}` ("kid" until Lead, then "partner"), `{lider}` and `{portador}` (oracle name), `{cacife}` (number), `{mao_n}` (hand number).
- **Addressing the player:** the title (Extra, Stunt Double, Supporting Role, Lead, Star, Midnight Legend) or fixed nicknames: "tadpole" (Frog), "creature" (Snake), "kid"/"partner" (Fox), "our guest" (third person). English is mostly gender-neutral already; keep it that way (no "buddy", "girl", "man").
- **Names:** O Grande Blefe = *The Big Bluff*; Sessão da Meia-Noite = *The Midnight Show*; Corte Final = *Final Cut*; cacife = *stack*; ficha (casino currency) = *chip*; Empréstimo do Sapo = *Frog Loan*; Intervalo = *Intermission*; "Anteriormente em…" = *Previously on…*; Freguês = *Easy Mark*; Nêmesis = *Nemesis*. Bogart calls Fincher "Gramps" and Cypher "the Snake"; Cypher calls Bogart "froggy" and Fincher "old man"; Fincher calls Bogart "Critic" and Cypher "my dear" or "viper".
- **Cue and tell rules** are identical to the Portuguese bank (header of `banco-v1.md`): the Frog says nothing on `calado`; B26 only on `nervoso`; the Frog's all-in lines only when short; the Fox never speaks while showing the smoke ring; the Snake's words never state her hand strength.
